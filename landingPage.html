Crafting Flavors Since 2020

Welcome to Dharag , where passion meets the plate. What started as a small kitchen experiment has grown into a local favorite for food lovers who crave authenticity and bold flavors.

Whether it’s our hand-spun milkshakes, our signature pizzas, or the perfect shawarma, we believe in using only the freshest ingredients sourced from local farmers. Every bite is designed to tell a story of quality and care.
